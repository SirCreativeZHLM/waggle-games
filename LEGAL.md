# Legal and originality notes

Waggle Games is an independent project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Nintendo or any other
console maker. All trademarks belong to their owners.

This is not legal advice, but these are the choices made to keep the project clearly original:

- **Names.** The project, the ten games, the controller app and the characters all have original names. No
  console, franchise or character names are used in code, UI or assets.
- **Characters.** Players are eggs, not avatars resembling any existing character design.
- **Assets.** There are no imported art, audio, font or model files. Every model is assembled from primitive shapes in
  GDScript; every sound and the menu loop are synthesised at runtime. The UI uses Godot's bundled default font (MIT/OFL).
- **Code.** Written from scratch; no decompiled or leaked source, and no emulation. No game ROMs, SDKs or firmware
  are used or needed.
- **Mechanics.** Sports and motion-pointing are generic ideas and not protectable. Rules, layouts, scoring and
  visuals here are our own implementations.
- **Hardware.** The remote is an ordinary phone app speaking our own UDP protocol. It does not mimic any
  proprietary controller protocol.

If you contribute, do not add assets or code taken from commercial games.

Godot Engine is MIT licensed (https://godotengine.org/license).
